# AI Operations Specialist

This repository contains my practical work, tasks, and projects completed during my **AI Operations Specialist Training at Mayerfeld Consulting**.

The repository documents my hands-on learning journey, including AI concepts, workflow automation, practical tasks, and the use of AI tools to develop and improve business processes.

## Training Overview

The training focuses on applying AI to real-world business and operational processes, with an emphasis on automation, workflow design, and practical AI solutions.
